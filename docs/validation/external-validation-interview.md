## Interview script (facilitator)

1. **Role:** What Lean project and role do you work in?
2. **Bottleneck:** What computational obligation currently slows proofs or tooling?
3. **Workaround:** What do you do today (CAS dump, trust-the-solver, skip, other)?
4. **Fit:** Does MathEvidence’s “adapters untrusted; Lean checker trusted” model match a real need?
5. **Capability:** Which MathEvidence capability did you try (or N/A if interview-only)?
6. **Impact:** Any observed or expected time / proof-complexity impact?
7. **Trust concerns:** Semantic gaps, digest binding, replay, or claim-strength worries?
8. **Consent:** May we quote you, anonymize, or neither?

Record answers in the slot tables below. Leave empty until a real session ends.
